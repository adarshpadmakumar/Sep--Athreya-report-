✓ September opened as the strongest stretch on record

From September 1 to 11, organic search averaged 156 clicks a day at a 4.16% click-through rate. Both are the highest of any period since reporting began in May, and the 6.75 average position matched June's best. That stretch includes two of the three highest days since May: 181 clicks on September 1 and 177 on September 4.

✦ Late September and October are holding above August

From Sep 28 to Oct 25 the site averaged 114 clicks and 3,419 impressions a day. Clicks per day are 2.2% above August and impressions 6.1% above, and average position improved by more than half a rank (7.49 to 6.92). Weekly clicks were steady between 767 and 861. The early-September surge has eased, so this is the baseline the October and November work builds on.

October – November 2026
Action plan
Ordered by impact on clicks and leads. Each item builds on work already under way.
1

Fix appointment form completion and set up lead tracking

In September, 51 visitors started the enquiry form but only 3 submissions were recorded, all from one user. That points to either a broken submission or a broken tracking event. We'll test the form end to end, then mark form submissions, contact clicks, phone taps and WhatsApp taps as GA4 key events. Today no key events are configured, so every lead the site produces goes unmeasured.

Impact: LeadsWeek 1
2

Rewrite search snippets for the 10 under-clicked pages

New titles, meta descriptions and FAQ blocks for Neurology, Cosmetic Dermatology, Urology, About Us, Facilities, O&G, Gastroenterology, Endocrinology, and the two top articles. Neurology comes first: it ranks at 3.87 but converts only 1.13% of 9,209 impressions.

Impact: +490 clicks / 28 daysWeeks 1–2
3

Publish the rebuilt department pages and complete the last two

Urology, O&G, Orthopaedics and ENT are drafted. ENT goes live after its duplicate URL is consolidated with a 301 redirect. Gastroenterology and Endocrinology are drafted next, because their searches rank at 9.4–9.5 with almost no clicks today.

Impact: RankingsWeeks 2–5
4

Strengthen AI visibility on question-led content

Add answer-first summaries, FAQPage and MedicalWebPage schema, and author and reviewer credentials to the articles that already rank for question searches (psychiatrist vs psychologist, spine MRI report, when to see a neurologist). We'll track the AI Assistant channel in GA4 each month as the measure of progress.

Impact: AI citationsWeeks 2–4
5

Continue the content calendar

Two agency-written articles a month, focused on the specialties with the strongest search demand: hair loss, heart attack vs panic attack, piles treatment and snoring/sleep apnea are next. Each one links to its department page and the relevant doctor profiles.

Impact: New visibilityOngoing
6

Local and off-page authority

Run the October off-page plan: set up and optimize the Google Business Profile, and build consistent local citations. This targets broad local searches such as "hospitals in Thrissur" (642 impressions at position 8.0) and "hospital near me" (1,041 impressions at 6.5).

Impact: Local rankingsOngoing
7

Clean up malformed URLs and filter non-human traffic

17 malformed URL paths (for example /vascular-surgery/nutritional-therapy and /departments/undefined) logged 95 page views in September. We'll redirect or remove them. Singapore traffic (282 users, 1.8% engagement, 1.3s average time) behaves like automated traffic, and filtering it out of GA4 will give cleaner audience reporting from October onward.

Impact: Crawl health + data qualityWeeks 3–4
The bottom line for September

Organic search grew its share of Atreya's audience, with 15.3% more new users from Google than in August and 73% of all new visitors. Patient-education articles that weren't visible before now earn thousands of impressions, and several department and doctor pages moved up in rankings. Search clicks in late September and October are holding above August, though below the early-September peak. The next gains come from turning page-one visibility into clicks and measuring every enquiry those clicks produce.
